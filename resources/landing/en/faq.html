An Ethical AI shoot is a shoot without a physical shoot day: only your likeness is used to produce the photos and/or videos. That also means there's no day fee, just a buy-out. Why do we call it ethical? Because you're still the model being cast and paid, instead of being replaced by AI altogether, and we always aim for a fair buy-out that matches what a real shoot would pay. There's also a hybrid Ethical AI shoot: a physical shoot combined with content or visuals that are produced with AI.

In an Ethical AI shoot, a few things matter extra. Who gets to create the AI likeness (the training model), and who owns it? In every case, that's you, the talent. The client may only use that model with your consent, and only for the agreed shoot. That's always spelled out clearly: which shoot it is, how many visuals will be made, whether it's photos and/or videos, and a maximum number. A buy-out is attached to those visuals, just like at a traditional shoot, for a period of one or two years depending on the channels and the continents, countries or regions, so everything happens properly. On top of that, you always have the right to have the data deleted. And crucially: all of this comes back in the contract. That can be one main contract with annexes, or even several contracts when more parties are involved.

Consent works much like a normal shoot: you give permission for a set number of visuals, for a specific purpose, channel, campaign and duration. The difference: at a real shoot it's often unclear how many photos will be taken, while at an AI shoot we want the number of visuals nailed down up front. Compensation is tied to the use of the images or videos: just like at most traditional shoots, the buy-out kicks in the moment the campaign goes live. If the visuals never go live, the contract always includes an amount that compensates the time you spent supplying your photos; and at a hybrid shoot you receive your half-day or full-day fee. Either way, you're always compensated fairly for the time you put in.
